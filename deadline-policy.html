Deadlines and Extensions: Read Before You Submit

Posted deadlines

Submit through Blackboard by the posted deadline. All dates and times use Eastern Time. Saving a draft is not the same as submitting; check for Blackboard's submission confirmation.

Regular assignments: 48 hours without a penalty

Regular assignments have an automatic 48-hour submission window after the posted deadline, without a grade penalty. You do not need permission to use this window. Each assessment shows its posted deadline and the exact date and time the window ends. Blackboard may label a submission made during this window as late; that label does not mean a penalty applies.

Request an extension BEFORE the posted deadline

Routine extension requests must reach me before the original posted deadline. The 48-hour submission window does not extend the deadline for requesting an extension. Routine requests made during or after that window will not be approved. A request made before the deadline does not guarantee approval.

After the 48-hour window

Work submitted after the standard 48-hour window receives zero unless an extension or exception has been approved. Blackboard may still allow an upload so approved exceptions can be handled; an available submission box does not extend the acceptance deadline.

Unexpected emergencies and accommodations

If an unexpected emergency arises, or prevents you from contacting me earlier, contact me as soon as possible. Exceptional circumstances are considered individually. Approved accommodations remain in effect and determine your applicable arrangements. Contact me privately rather than posting personal information in the course-question forum.

Scheduled live assessments

Scheduled live assessments or presentation components do not have an automatic 48-hour window. Contact me before the scheduled time if you cannot attend, or as soon as possible if an emergency prevents advance contact. Any written or recorded alternative follows the submission instructions for that assessment.

Final projects and the Final Learning Reflection

Final projects are due December 11, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern. The Final Learning Reflection is due December 13, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern. These final assessments have no automatic 48-hour window. Routine extension requests must arrive before the posted deadline. Approved accommodations and exceptional circumstances are handled individually.

If Blackboard prevents submission

Contact me promptly and keep evidence of the problem, such as the error message and time. Email is for reporting the issue, not the normal assessment submission route. I will explain how to proceed if a technical problem requires another arrangement.
