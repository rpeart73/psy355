How to use the course website

Updated September 12, 2026.

Open the course website Download the Word guide Print-friendly guide
  1. Open your week
  2. Read and explore
  3. Practise and save

Start with your week

Home
Open Home to see the weekly learning path. Choose the week you are studying.
Weekly Experiences
Open the weekly experience or week page. Begin with its purpose and learning outcomes, then follow its readings, media and learning activities.
Course Calendar
Check class dates, journal release dates and due dates. All times are Eastern. Open Blackboard for journal instructions, submission details and approved changes.

Read, watch and connect

Readings and Media
Browse the course sources and use the available search and filters. Open a source record for its description and access route.
Compare Sources
Select readings to compare their ideas. Use the comparison to notice connections and differences; return to the original sources for evidence.
Videos and Podcasts
Choose a media item and use captions or a transcript when available. Some sources open on another website.

Practise the course concepts

Glossary
Look up a term when you need a clear definition or want to revisit an earlier concept.
Concept Flashcards
Use the flashcards to practise recalling concepts. Return to the readings when an idea needs more explanation.
Weekly learning tools
Use the starting check to identify ideas you want to revisit; it records your self-rating, not a grade. After a knowledge check, read the explanation for each answer and follow the suggested next step. These tools do not submit work to Blackboard.

Keep your notes

Find your work
Open My notes in the menu. Choose a week to continue writing. Your saved entries are grouped by activity and week; use the search box to find an idea.
Check the save message
Notes save automatically in this browser on this device. Wait for Saved in this browser. If the message says Not saved, keep the page open and download a copy before leaving.
Keep an organized copy
In My notes, choose Download all organized notes (.docx). The Word file keeps your wording and groups your notes with their week or activity.
Move to another device
Choose Download backup for another device (.json). On the other device, open this same course, go to My notes and choose Restore from a backup file. Notes do not sync automatically between devices.
Recover another version
If two tabs edit the same note, look under Other saved versions for the alternate wording. Check that text before replacing your current note.
Shared devices
Download your work before using Clear My Work or clearing browser data. Avoid relying on private browsing to keep notes after the session ends.

If you get stuck

Navigation
Use the site menu to move between the weekly pages and learning tools. On a small screen, open the menu if it is collapsed.
Reading access
Follow the access instructions shown with each source. Some readings require Blackboard or the Seneca Library.
Broken link or missing media
Use Report a problem to prepare a message, or contact the instructor through Blackboard. Include the course, week and page that needs attention.

Assignments stay in Blackboard

Open Blackboard for every assignment, its instructions, grading criteria and submission area. This guide and the public website do not provide assignment downloads.
